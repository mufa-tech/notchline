How the numbers are checked

Every formula in the app has test cases with a source. Here are the published values the free calculators on this site reproduce, and how the site is held to the app.

The engine

The stair and roof maths runs in one engine, tested with 353 cases, 91 of them against published worked examples: carpentry references, code commentary, trade schools and stair-maker guides. A failing case stops a release.

This site against the app

The calculators on this site are a port of the same engine to JavaScript. Every change runs them against 2079 stairs across 11 rule sets and 318 roof and birdsmouth cases computed by the app's own engine: every number must match to a billionth and every rule must reach the same verdict.
